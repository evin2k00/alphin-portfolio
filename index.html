The Foundation

Economics established the method.

I’m an economist and entrepreneur, and my work sits at the intersection of finance, technology and design. I hold a degree in Economics from Christ College Autonomous, Irinjalakuda, Thrissur, and I’m currently pursuing a part time second degree in English and History.

My career has been built on a simple conviction: that the future of money and markets should be understood, not feared.

That conviction has taken shape across three ventures and two community roles. Cryptac, where I began educating people on crypto trading and protecting them from scams. Enovex, the startup ecosystem hub I founded to help entrepreneurs turn ideas into ventures. OrentoCorp, where I’m building what I call “the future of capital markets” through blockchain technology.

Alongside these, I serve as Director at Leaf NGO and President of Vyjayanthi Arts & Sports Club, extending my leadership beyond business into community life.
